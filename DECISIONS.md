## 2026-11-24 (tests)
- **HCLTech share-table fix:** Segment EBIT-margin tables reuse the segment labels, so some quarters picked up margins (~17%) as shares. Share sets are now accepted only when they sum to 100 ± 1 within a single table. HCLTech segment shares for Jun 2022–Jun 2023 are therefore NA. The new geography scheme (USA/Europe/ROW/India, services basis) is tagged `[svc]` so its Europe/ROW never mixes with the old Americas/Europe/RoW. H3 uses old-scheme Americas/Europe through Mar 2025, then USA/Europe[svc], with firm × definition fixed effects.
- **H3 specification:** With two regions per firm-quarter, the pre-registered regression (seg × post, firm × seg FE, firm × quarter FE) is algebraically the same as regressing log(NA share ÷ Europe share) on post with firm × definition FE. SEs are clustered by company (LTI and LTIMindtree = one cluster; 6 clusters, so p-values are rough). TCS constant-currency regional growth is used as the currency check.
- **H6 deviation:** The pre-registration says constant currency. Revenue per employee is computed in US$ because no constant-currency *level* series exists for all firms. Most revenue is billed in US$; currency is flagged as a caveat. LTIMindtree is excluded from H6 trend fits (only 22 quarters, starting FY22); LTI standalone has no post-period.
- **H4 method:** Hires are implied as net change + attrition × average headcount ÷ 4, using each firm's own attrition definition. Comparisons are within firm only. "Unusually high attrition" = above the firm's 2016–19 mean + 2 SD. Layoff windows come from `data/processed/layoff_events.csv` (TCS only: no other formal layoff announcement by the six firms was found in 2024–26 searches).
- **H2 fallbacks:** Accenture from the "TYPE OF WORK" tables in 43 8-K earnings releases (local-currency YoY growth on a restated year-ago basis, so the FY23 rename from Outsourcing to Managed Services doesn't break comparability; FY20 Q1–Q2 releases didn't match the table pattern and are NA). TechM BPS vs. software headcount; HCLTech ITBS vs. ERS implied revenue (HCLSoftware excluded because it's lumpy with IBM-product deals). Pooled with Newey-West (4 lags), since 3 series can't be clustered. BEA buckets: exposed = computer services + accounting/audit/bookkeeping + legal (Philippines: telecom/computer/info, because computer services alone is suppressed); unexposed = R&D + architecture/engineering/technical + business & management consulting/PR.
- **Philippines BEA reclassification flag:** In 2022 Philippine telecom/computer/info imports fell by ~480 while business & management consulting rose by ~1,450. That looks like a category reclassification (likely contact-center services), so Philippines-by-type results are not treated as evidence.
- **H1 robustness:** Trend windows 2015–21 and 2015–19 added. India is above trend in both 2015–22 and 2015–21, and on trend with the pre-COVID window (where it trails the benchmark by ~18 pp). Philippines results change sign across windows, so they are not robust.
- **H5 robustness:** The 2015–19 window gives the same answer (NAICS 5415 −14% below trend in 2026).
